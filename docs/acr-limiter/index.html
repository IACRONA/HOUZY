ACR LIMITER · 1.0

Four stages, each where it belongs.

AMOUNT raises the level, then the chain catches the peaks in order: ACR takes the short peaks above ~4 kHz, T8 / T9 holds the bass, the kick body and the middle, TRUE PEAK guards the ceiling between samples.

The clipper always comes before the limiter. Bass never goes into the clipper, so it never crunches; the highs never go into a slow limiter, so they never pump.

CEILING, CHARACTER and OUTPUT set the rest. BYPASS is aligned to the latency, so you compare the same moment.

One gain for the whole peak.

An ordinary limiter turns the whole track down whenever any peak is too loud:

  • The kick ducks the highs — a bass peak pulls the hats and the vocal down with it.
  • Grit inside every hit — the gain moves in the middle of a bass wave and bends it.
  • Dull top end — a hard clipper slices the waveform and its error lands across the whole spectrum.
  • Inter-sample overs — the export clips after MP3 or streaming encoding.

ACR — a clipper that stops chopping the highs.

A slice is abrupt and its error is broadband: every kick hit subtracts that error from the hats, the vocal and the tails.

ACR subtracts a short pulse placed exactly at the peak. Its spectrum puts the distortion at the top, where the hats and the attack mask it. The depth grows with AMOUNT: 0.5 dB at the start, up to 2 dB at full drive.

T8 — the half-wave limiter.

T8 changes its gain only where the wave crosses zero. Each half-wave of the kick and bass is lowered as one whole piece, so its shape stays exactly as it was. Only its height changes.

Six bands with linear phase: a bass peak never ducks the highs, each band is limited on its own.

T9 — the envelope limiter for the low end.

T9 reads the envelope of the bass instead of single waves: the gain holds the whole top of a note and lets it go smoothly, with no flutter on long sub notes.

T8 | T9 is one switch, the change crossfades in 10 ms without a gap. T9 is the default.

Everything a master limiter needs.

  • TRUE PEAK — on by default, for streaming and MP3 exports.
  • CEILING — −12…0 dB.
  • CHARACTER — light saturation before the limiter.
  • OUTPUT — level after the limiter.
  • LINEAR | NATURAL | CLEAN L — phase of the bands.
  • OVERSAMPLE — 4x to 64x, 8x by default.
  • MIX & MASTER — the catalogue of mixing and mastering engineers inside the plugin.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes: the free trial runs 14 days with everything unlocked. To keep it, buy a licence on Gumroad: one payment, a lifetime licence for two computers.

What is the difference between T8 and T9?

Both hold the bass and the kick body. T8 changes its gain only where the wave crosses zero, so every half-wave keeps its shape. T9 follows the envelope of the bass and lets long sub notes go smoothly. T9 is the default; switch and choose by ear.

Can I use it together with HOUZY?

Yes. Put ACR LIMITER after HOUZY on the master: HOUZY evens the compression, ACR LIMITER sets the final loudness and the ceiling. They are separate plugins with separate licences.

Does my audio leave my computer?

Never. All processing happens on your machine. The plugin goes online only to check for updates and to activate your licence. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Which systems and DAWs are supported?

VST3 on Windows 10/11, 64-bit: Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper, Bitwig and other VST3 hosts. On macOS 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel): VST3 and AU, Logic Pro included.

How much latency does it add?

About 0.1 s (the look-ahead of the limiter and the linear-phase bands). The plugin reports it to the DAW, which compensates it, so the tracks stay in time.

How does the online demo work?

It runs the real ACR LIMITER engine in your browser. Load an MP3 or WAV: the first 75 seconds play in a loop. The track never leaves your computer.
