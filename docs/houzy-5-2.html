HOUZY 5.2

What's new · 9 November 2026

HOUZY II: an engine rebuilt from the ground up

HOUZY II arrived in the last release. In 5.2 we have essentially rebuilt it.

The bands now work as one team. Each part of the spectrum used to be squeezed on its own: the loud bass got pushed down while the mids lived their own life. Now the bands are linked. When the bass is held down, the mids and highs get exactly as much compression as the track needs to stay in balance. That is the evenness you hear straight away.

Tone under control for the whole song. HOUZY II watches the tonal balance over whole sections of the track, not just the current moment, and it remembers how the verse and the chorus sounded. Compression no longer colours the sound: warm stays warm, bright stays bright.

It hears ahead. The engine listens a quarter of a second into the future, so the entry of the hats or a drop is met prepared instead of squashed late.

The attack of every hit is kept. A new attack guard holds the punch where it matters most without dulling the top.

The level no longer drifts. AUTO GAIN in HOUZY II brings the loudness back smoothly and calmly, with no breathing between loud and quiet passages. Turn a knob and it follows quickly; let go and it holds steady.

An even low end. LOW and SUB are now compressed in step, so the bass no longer splits into "booming below, empty above".

And a word on dynamics: with AUTO GAIN on, HOUZY evens the track out without eating its dynamics. Loudness, the distance between quiet and loud, the sense of air all stay in your hands.

CLEAN L: the clarity of LINEAR, a kick that hits

A new third mode on the phase key. LINEAR gives you crystal transparency; CLEAN L keeps all of it and lets the kick step forward. You hear a crisp click first, and the bass follows without smearing it. The tonal balance doesn't move. It sounds just as transparent, only the hit is more distinct.

If the kick matters in your music (where doesn't it?), try this one first.

BEAT and SMART: a release that does what it says

Both release modes have been reworked.

  • BEAT now lets go in exactly the time you set. It used to get only partway back within that time and needed about three times longer to finish. Now "234 ms" means 234 ms. BEAT is the new default and sits in every preset.
  • SMART has learned to catch fading sounds. When a tone dies away, SMART lets go of it earlier instead of holding compression on something that is no longer there. On slow decays it used to do almost nothing. Now you hear it.

Limiter and oversampling

The limiter is simpler: T6 and T8, with T8 as the default. Oversampling now defaults to 8x, which measured the same as 16x at a lower CPU cost.

The oversampler now switches itself on only when the safety clipper actually has something to cut. Until then it does not run at all and does not touch the sound, and it never misses a single cut.

2.5 times lighter on the CPU

This is probably the change you will enjoy without touching anything.

HOUZY 5.2 uses 2.5 times less CPU: from about 12 % of a core to under 5 %. At small buffer sizes, where every spike counts, the worst block now takes a third of the budget instead of three quarters. More instances per project, fewer clicks at low latency, a quieter laptop.

No compromises either. Most of the speed-up does not change the sound by a single bit, and where it does, the difference sits below −120 dB, far beyond hearing. Measured, not assumed.

MIX & MASTER: engineers inside the plugin

The biggest new thing in HOUZY 5.2 isn't about compression at all. It's about people.

Press MIX & MASTER on the panel and a catalogue of mixing and mastering engineers who work with HOUZY opens up.

  • Listen to their work right in the plugin. Every engineer has a track in full quality. Press play and hear it in the same place you mix your own, and nothing of it ends up in your export.
  • Find your sound. Filter by style, search by nickname; every card has a colour of its own.
  • Talk directly. A built-in live chat: messages are encrypted, you can send a voice message or a track, translate a message into your language, pin, delete for both, block. A badge on the panel tells you when something new arrives.
  • Become an engineer yourself. Fill in a profile, upload your work, and get found.

The catalogue is visible in the trial too; chat and contacts open with a licence.

And plenty of smaller things

  • The HOUZY and HOUZY II tooltips now explain how the engines differ, and the manual has a detailed section on them.
  • Presets are re-tuned for the new engine; SMOOTH has been dialled in again.
  • AMOUNT starts at 0: HOUZY changes nothing until you ask it to.
  • A bug report button right in the plugin, and announcements from ACRONA AUDIO inside it.
  • The ALL MIX band knobs turn more precisely, and Ctrl gives you very fine adjustment.
  • A new default look: the PULIK theme with the MINT sphere.
