This policy explains what this website stores in your browser and what it loads from other companies. The short version: no tracking, no advertising, no analytics cookies.

What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar browser feature. European law (the ePrivacy Directive and its national versions, such as PECR in the UK) treats both the same way: anything that is not strictly necessary needs your consent first.

How to change your choice

  • Click Cookie settings at the bottom of any page and choose again. If you switch from Accept to Necessary only, the page reloads without the Google font.
  • Or clear this site's data in your browser settings. The banner will appear again on your next visit.
  • You can also block third-party content entirely with your browser or an extension. The site keeps working.

The HOUZY plugin is not a website and uses no cookies. What it stores on your computer and what it sends online is described in the Privacy Policy.
