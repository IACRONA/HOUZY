This policy explains what this website stores in your browser and what it loads from other companies. The short version: no tracking, no advertising, no analytics cookies.

What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar browser feature. European law (the ePrivacy Directive and its national versions, such as PECR in the UK) treats both the same way: anything that is not strictly necessary needs your consent first.
