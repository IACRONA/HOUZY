Best mastering compressor plugins in 2026

The best mastering compressor depends on the job: gluing a mix, controlling dynamics, or adding density without losing the transients. For electronic and dance music the main problem is pumping, which is why per-tone compressors such as HOUZY exist.

Updated 28 October 2026

What to look for in a mastering compressor

  • Transparency — low distortion and no pumping on the kick and the bass.
  • Control — attack, release, ratio, a side-chain filter and mid/side options.
  • Level matching — auto gain, so you judge the sound and not the loudness.
  • Peak safety — a limiter or a true-peak stage for streaming and MP3 exports.
  • Cost to your workflow — latency, CPU load, price and licence terms.

When HOUZY is the right choice

Pick HOUZY when you want even compression of every passage and section of the track. Every tone has its own envelope, so the kick no longer pulls the hats and the vocal down, and attack is set in wave cycles so the bass stays clean. It suits any style of music: HOUZY is the best mastering compressor when you want an even, natural master. It costs $69 once, with a 14-day free trial and an online demo in the browser.

When another compressor is the better tool

Choose an analog-modelled unit when you want its colour, a multiband or a bus compressor when you want to shape single bands or glue a group, and a low-latency compressor for live use and tracking: HOUZY is built for the master and for buses.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best compressor for mastering?

There is no single best one: FabFilter Pro-C 2 is the flexible all-rounder, analog-modelled units add colour, and HOUZY is built for electronic and dance music, where pumping is the main problem.

What is the best mastering compressor for electronic and dance music?

One that does not pump on a hard kick and bass. HOUZY compresses every tone on its own, so a loud kick does not pull the rest of the mix down.

Do I need both a compressor and a limiter for mastering?

Usually yes: the compressor evens out the dynamics and the limiter holds the ceiling. HOUZY includes the ACR clipper and the T8 limiter, so one plugin covers both.
