Questions

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. It is free, you only need a Gumroad account.

How do I get paid?

Through Gumroad. Your commission is added to your Gumroad balance at the time of the sale and is paid out the same way as any Gumroad earnings.

What are the 30% calculated from?

From the price the buyer actually pays. If a discount code is used, it is 30% of the discounted price.

Do I need my own website?

No. A channel, a profile or a mailing list works too: you only need to share your link.
